# Unit 5 - Past simple
## Conspect
Regular verbs end in -ed:
- They **invited** us to their party, but we **decided** not to go.
- Laura **passed** her exam becouse she **studied** very hard.

The past simple doesn't ends in -ed with irregular verbs:
- write -> wrote | Mozart wrote more then 600 pieces of music.
- see -> saw | We saw Alice it town a few days ago.
- go -> went | I went to the cinema three times last week.
- shut -> shut | It was cold, so I shut the window.

### Questions and negative sentences
In questions and negative sentences we use **did/didn't** + infinitive (enjoy/see/go etc.):
- Questions:
	- Did you enjoy?
	- Did she see?
	- Did they go?
- Negative sentences:
	- I didn't enjoy.
	- She didn't see
	- They didn't go.

### The past of **be** is **was/were**

| I, he, she, it | We, you, they  |
| -------------- | -------------- |
| Was / wasn't   | Were / weren't |

- **I was** annoyed becouse **they were** late.
- **Was the wether** good when **you were** on holiday?
- I wasn't hungry, so i didn't eat anything.
