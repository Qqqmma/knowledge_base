## Conspect

Some words are not normally used in continuous form. We don't say 'I am knowing', 'they are liking'. We say 'I know', 'they like'.
The following verbs are not normally used in present continuous:
- like
- want
- need
- prefer ?
- know
- realise ?
- understand
- recognise ?
- believe
- suppose
- remember
- mean
- belong ?
- fit ?
- contain ?
- consist ?
- seem ?

Examples:
- I am hungry. I want something to eat.
- Do you understend what i mean?
- Anna doesn't seem very happy right now.

### Think
When think means **'believe' or 'have an option'**, we **don't use** the continuous:
- I **think** Mary is Canadian, but i am not sure.
- What do you **think** of my idea? (=what is your option?)

When think means **'consider'**, the continuous is possible:
- **I'm thinking** about what happened. I often think about it.
- Nicky **is thinking** of giving up her job.

### See, hear, smell, taste, look, feel
We normally use present simple (not continuous) with **see/hear/smell/taste**:
- Do you see that man over there?
- The room smells. Let's open a window.
- This soup doesn't taste very good.

You can use the present simpoe or continuous to say how somebody **looks or feels now**:
- You **look** well today. *or* You **are looking** well today.
- How **do you fell** now? *or* How **are you felling** now?
