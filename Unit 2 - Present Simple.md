# Unit 2 - Present Simple
## Conspect

| I/they/we/you | drive/work/do etc.     |
| ------------- | ---------------------- |
| he/she/it     | drives/works/does etc. |


We use Present Simple to talk about things in general. We use it to say something happens all the time or repeatedly, or that something is true in general:
- The earth goes rount the sun.
- The caffee opens at 7:30 in the morning.

We use Present Simple to say how often we do things:
- I get up at 8 o'clock **every morning**.
- **How often** do you go to the dantist?
- Julie doesn't dring tea **very often**.
- Robert **usualy** **goes** away **two or three times a year.**

Sometimes we do thing by saying something. 
- I promise I won't be late.
- What do you suggest I do?

### Questions and negative sentences
We use **do/does** to make questions and negative sentences:
- I come from Canada. Where **do** you **come** from?
- I **don't** **go** away very often.
- What **does** this word mean?
- Rice **doesn't** grow up in cold climates.
